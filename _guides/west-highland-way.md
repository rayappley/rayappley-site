## Day by day

I planned six days and walked it in seven. Day 2 broke me, so I cut the rest of the route into shorter days, started walking at 7 every morning and took a 10-minute break every 3 km.

| Day | Section | Distance | Where I slept |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Milngavie → Conic Hill | 26 km | Tent by a stream, just before Conic Hill |
| 2 | Conic Hill → Inversnaid | 28 km | Tent outside the Inversnaid Hotel |
| 3 | Inversnaid → Crianlarich | 20 km | Tent about 2 km before Crianlarich |
