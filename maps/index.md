## What's in a map

Each map is a file you import into **maps.me**, **Organic Maps** or **Gaia GPS** before you go, plus a printable overview. It covers the whole trail, split into sections from village to village, with:

- water sources and stream crossings
- camp spots and campsites
- guesthouses, huts and shelters
- cafés, bars and shops
- passes, viewpoints, bus stops and ATMs

## Checked and unchecked points

Every point says whether I checked it myself and when. Points I did not see in person come from OpenStreetMap and are marked as unchecked, so you know which ones to rely on.
