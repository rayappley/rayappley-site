_Last updated: November 2026_

This site is run by Réka (rayappley). It's a simple blog: there are no accounts, comments, newsletter, contact forms or ads, and I don't use cookies or analytics.

## What is collected when you visit

- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub's servers receive technical data such as your IP address, your browser and the page you asked for, and may keep it in their logs. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

I don't see or store any of this data myself.

## Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites, like Instagram or booking sites. Once you click through, their own privacy policies apply.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your personal data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, and you can complain to a data protection authority. In Hungary, that's the [NAIH](https://naih.hu). For any privacy question, write to me at [rayappley@gmail.com](mailto:rayappley@gmail.com).
